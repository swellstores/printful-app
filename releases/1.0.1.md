First release of the Printful integration for Swell.

- **Live shipping rates at checkout.** Printful quotes shipping for the shopper's address and cart, listed alongside your own shipping services. Quotes are cached, and an optional fallback rate is offered when Printful is slow or rate-limited.
- **Automatic order submission.** Paid orders that contain products mapped to Printful are sent to Printful with their print technique and artwork.
- **Tracking back on the order.** When Printful ships, the shipment and its tracking number are added to the Swell order.
- **Curated catalog.** The Printful catalog syncs nightly into a table you map your product variants against. Nothing becomes a product in your store until you choose it.
- **Print setup on each product.** A Printful tab on the product holds the print technique, artwork and placements.
- **Safe by default.** Live fulfillment starts off. Until you turn it on, orders reach Printful as unconfirmed drafts, so nothing is printed or charged.
- **Problems where you work.** If Printful rejects an order or a print file fails its checks, the reason appears on the Swell order and your store admins get an email. Set the fulfillment job back to Pending to retry.

**Getting started:** create a Printful private token with the `orders`, `catalog` and `webhooks` scopes and paste it into the app settings, then enable Printful under Settings → Shipping.
