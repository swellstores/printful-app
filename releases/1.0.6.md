Use Printful with products that already exist in your Swell store.

- **Products start in Swell or Printful.** A new setting chooses where your catalog starts. With **Swell**, the app never creates products: it links the ones you already have to their Printful products instead.
- **Linked automatically.** A Printful product whose external ID names a Swell product, bare or as `swell:{id}`, is linked at the next sync, and so are its variants.
- **Or link by hand.** On a product page, choose **Actions → Link to Printful product** and enter the Printful product's ID or dashboard link, or leave it empty to use the Printful product with the same name. **Unlink from Printful** undoes it.
- **Your product stays yours.** The sync only links a linked product's variants, by external ID or by matching sizes and colors (12″×16″ matches 12x16). It never changes its options, prices, names or photos.
- **Design once in Printful.** Orders are printed from the file and placement saved on the Printful product, whatever photos the Swell product shows.
- **Products without variants** can be linked as a whole to a Printful product with one variant.
