Products now come from Printful. Design them there and the app creates them in Swell.

- **Products sync from Printful.** Products you design in a Printful Manual order platform / API store are created in Swell automatically, with their colors and sizes as variants, Printful's mockups as photos and your retail prices. They arrive as drafts for you to review. The sync runs every hour.
- **Sync on demand.** Choose Sync products now on the app page, or Sync from Printful in the Products list, to pull new designs in right away. The sync runs in the background, and the app's Runs tab shows its progress. Resync from Printful re-reads a product, or the products you select, and can replace their photos with Printful's latest mockups.
- **Stays in step.** Variants added in Printful appear in Swell; removed or discontinued ones are taken off sale, and so are products deleted in Printful. Your edits to names, descriptions, prices and photos in Swell are kept.
- **Orders use your Printful design.** Paid orders reference the product you designed, so Printful prints the files stored with it. There is no artwork to set up in Swell.
- **Live shipping rates at checkout,** with an optional fallback rate when Printful is slow.
- **Safe by default.** Live fulfillment starts off: orders reach Printful as unconfirmed drafts, so nothing is printed or charged until you turn it on.
- **Tracking and problems on the order.** Shipments and tracking numbers are added to the Swell order within about 10 minutes of shipping, with nothing to register in Printful. If Printful rejects an order, it is marked failed on the order and your store admins get an email; set the fulfillment job back to Pending to retry.

**Getting started:** in Printful, add a Manual order platform / API store and design your products there. Create a private token for that store with the `orders` and `sync_products` scopes, paste it into the app settings and choose Actions → Sync products now, then enable Printful under Settings → Shipping.
