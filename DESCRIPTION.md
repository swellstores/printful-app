Sell your own designs on shirts, mugs, posters and hats without buying a single blank. Printful makes each item when someone orders it and ships it under your brand. This app connects Printful to your Swell store, so an order goes from checkout to tracking number without you copying it anywhere.

Pick a product from Printful's catalog, point your Swell variant at it, and add your artwork. From then on, every paid order is sent to Printful automatically, and the shipment and tracking code come back to the Swell order when it ships.

- **Live rates at checkout.** Shoppers see Printful's real shipping prices for their cart and address. Quotes are cached so checkout stays fast, and you can set a flat fallback rate for when Printful is slow to answer.
- **Hands-off orders.** Paid orders go to Printful on their own. Tracking lands on the Swell order as an ordinary shipment, so your storefront, order emails and customer order history show it with nothing extra to set up.
- **A catalog you curate.** The full Printful catalog syncs into your dashboard every night, but nothing becomes a product until you choose it. A merch shop sells six products, not five hundred, and you only list items you've seen print files for.
- **Print setup on the product.** A Printful tab on each product holds the print technique, the artwork, and placements for items that print in more than one spot, with the print area shown so you can size artwork to fit.

**Try it before it costs anything.** Live fulfillment starts switched off. Until you turn it on, orders reach Printful as unconfirmed drafts, so nothing is printed and nothing is charged. Place a test order, open the draft in Printful, check the sizes and print files, then flip the switch.

**Problems show up where you work.** If Printful rejects an order or a print file fails its checks, the reason appears on the Swell order and you get an email. A Failed view lists every stuck submission, and retrying one takes a single field change. When an order is canceled or refunded at Printful, you're told about it, and your Swell order is left for you to decide.

**Works with any storefront.** The app adds no storefront of its own and needs none. Rates, shipments and tracking all live on standard Swell records, so any theme or storefront app you sell through shows them without knowing the app is installed.

Setup takes a few minutes. Paste a Printful API token, enable Printful under Settings → Shipping, map the products you want to sell, and add their artwork.
